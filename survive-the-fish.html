Survive the Fish

A tower defense game built in Godot with C#. Fish swim between a chain of islands towards your base — place towers like the Fisherman to take them out before they get through, earning money to build up your defences between waves.

How it works

  • Fish spawn in waves and path through the water between islands — hit "Start Wave" when you're ready for the next one.
  • Defeating fish earns money, spent on placing towers such as the Fisherman around the islands.
  • Hearts in the top bar track your lives — each fish that gets past costs you one.
  • Run out of lives and it's game over.
