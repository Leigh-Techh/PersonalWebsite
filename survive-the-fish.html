Survive the Fish

A browser-based tower defense game built with HTML5 canvas and vanilla JavaScript — no libraries, no frameworks. Place cannons, fishermen, and patrol boats along the coastline to stop waves of fish reaching the shore.

Open in a new tab ↗

How it works

  • Fish spawn in waves and swim along a fixed path toward your base — each wave gets tougher.
  • Gold earned from defeated fish buys three tower types: Cannon, Fisherman, and Patrol Boat, each with its own range, cost, and behaviour.
  • Click a tile to place the currently selected tower, as long as you've got the gold and the tile is free.
  • Lose all your lives and it's game over — click anywhere to return to the start menu and try again.
